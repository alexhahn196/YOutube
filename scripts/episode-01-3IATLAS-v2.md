### ▐ SEGMENT 2 — THE EVIDENCE  (4:00–~12:00)
*[VISUAL: On-Screen-Label „THE EVIDENCE". Nüchterner, daten-getriebener Look; echtes NASA/HST/JWST-Material einmischen.]*

> Okay. Let's look at what our best telescopes actually measured — because the real object is stranger than the clickbait, just not in the way you'd think. And the single most important word here is **timing**. Almost every "anomaly" was a snapshot of a frozen object — measured **before** it rounded the Sun.

> **Where it came from.** Astronomers traced its motion and found it belongs to the Milky Way's ancient **thick disk** — a population of stars far older than ours. The best estimate puts 3I/ATLAS at **more than 7.6 billion years old**, very likely **older than the Sun itself.** We are looking at a genuine relic from another part of the galaxy.
> *[>> KORREKTUR K8: „>7,6 Gyr, sehr wahrscheinlich älter als die Sonne" statt „10–12 Mrd. als Fakt / älter als die Erde". Quelle: Hopkins et al. 2025.]*

> **How fast, how close.** It reached its closest point to the Sun — perihelion — on **October 29th, 2025**, passing between the orbits of Earth and Mars. Near the Sun, gravity whipped it up to about **68 kilometers per second** — but remember, its real cruising speed between the stars is that **58**. It never came close to Earth. Its nearest pass by us was on **December 19th, 2025** — and it did nothing unusual: no course change, no maneuver, just a comet on a predictable path. It then rounded Jupiter in **March 2026**, and it's now heading back out into interstellar space.
> *[>> KORREKTUR K9 + K2: v∞ vs. Perihel-Spitze getrennt; 19.12.-Beat (größte Erdannäherung, kein Manöver) ergänzt.]*

> Now the anomalies — one at a time.

> **The backwards tail.** Normal comets grow a tail blown **away** from the Sun. 3I/ATLAS showed an **anti-tail**, pointing at times **toward** it. Spooky? Here's the thing: astronomers had **already seen this** in other comets. A comet called Bernardinelli–Bernstein showed the same sunward feature back in **2021** — explained by ordinary physics: large, heavy dust grains drifting off the sunlit side. Physicists Jason Wright and Eric Keto reproduced 3I/ATLAS's tail with plain comet behavior. Unusual geometry — not new, not unexplained.
> *[>> KORREKTUR K5: Präzedenz C/2014 UN271 (Farnham et al. 2021) + Wright & Keto ergänzt. Quelle: AstroWright 2025-09-29.]*

> **The inside-out chemistry.** When James Webb measured its gas cloud in **August 2025**, before perihelion, it was almost the **opposite** of a normal comet: about **87% carbon dioxide, 9% carbon monoxide, and only ~4% water.** That's real — but it's a snapshot of a **deep-frozen** object far from the Sun. Watch what happened when it warmed up. By **December 2025**, its water output had jumped roughly **forty-fold**; **methane** appeared that simply wasn't detectable before; and the carbon-dioxide-to-water ratio **collapsed** toward ordinary comet values. The "impossible chemistry" didn't survive contact with sunlight. It was never a permanent state — it was a cold object thawing out.
> *[>> KORREKTUR K3: 87/9/4 als prä-perihel-Snapshot (JWST Aug 2025) markiert; post-perihel Auflösung (H₂O ~40×, Methan neu, CO₂/H₂O gesunken) ergänzt. Quellen: arXiv 2508.18209; 2601.06759; 2603.25002; JWST MIRI.]*

> **The metal.** Early on, its gas showed **nickel but almost no iron** — genuinely odd; the highest nickel-to-iron ratio of any comet ever seen. And again — a snapshot. **After** perihelion, **iron showed up** right alongside the nickel, and the ratio fell into line with an ordinary solar-system comet, comet Tempel 1. The "missing iron" wasn't missing. It was just cold.
> *[>> KORREKTUR K4: „Ni ohne Fe" als prä-perihel markiert; post-perihel Fe I detektiert, Ni/Fe ≈ 9P/Tempel 1. Quellen: DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/ae73fe; arXiv 2605.07652.]*

> Now — to be fair, some of Loeb's points are **harder** to wave away, and honesty means naming them. Three stand out. *One:* 3I/ATLAS arrived from a direction that sits within **9 degrees** of the famous "Wow!" radio signal — a coincidence with a probability around 0.6%. *Two:* near the Sun it showed a small but real **non-gravitational push** — and unusually, part of it points **sideways**, not straight away from the Sun. *Three:* Loeb reports a set of **jets** in a suspiciously symmetric arrangement. These are the strongest cards on the table — and each has a proposed **natural** answer: the Wow! alignment is a geometric coincidence across a huge sky; the sideways push is what you'd expect from gas venting unevenly from a spinning, thawing comet, and peer-reviewed work argues normal outgassing can cover it; the jets trace active vents. Real anomalies — not proof of engineering.
> *[>> KORREKTUR K7: Strohmann behoben — starke Anomalien (Wow! 9°/p0,6 %; transversale nicht-grav. Beschleunigung; 3-Jet-Symmetrie) aufgenommen + natürliche Erklärungen mit Urheber. Quellen: Loeb Medium; arXiv 2511.07450 / 2603.00782.]*

> Which leaves the question everyone actually clicked for: **did anyone check if it's transmitting?** Yes — twice, and this is the strongest evidence of the whole story.

> First, the **Green Bank Telescope** — one of the largest on Earth — listened on December 18th, 2025, across a huge radio range. It found **nothing** artificial down to a power of **100 milliwatts.** Let that sink in: from **270 million kilometers** away, that search was sensitive enough to hear a signal weaker than a **cell phone.** If 3I/ATLAS were quietly broadcasting at anything like the power of our own everyday technology, we would have heard it. We didn't.
> Then the **SETI Institute's Allen Telescope Array** went further: over seven hours it logged nearly **74 million** candidate blips. Every promising one was re-checked — and **all** of them traced back to **human** technology on the ground or satellites in orbit. Signals coming **from** 3I/ATLAS: **zero.**
> *[>> KORREKTUR K1: v1-Erfindung („beim Wegschwenken tauchten dieselben wieder auf") entfernt. Richtig: Green Bank Nichtdetektion bis 100 mW EIRP (1–12 GHz) = der stärkste Beleg; ATA 74 Mio.→~200→alle irdisch/Satellit. Quellen: arXiv 2512.19763; AJ DOI 10.3847/1538-3881/ae6651.]*

> And Loeb himself? He rates it a four and calls it **"most likely natural."** Not a reversal — that's what a four on his scale meant all along. The broader community is clearer still: **no evidence** that 3I/ATLAS is anything but a natural — if genuinely unusual — comet.
