Death three. The backwards tail.

[BEAT]
And here is where Loeb ran into a problem. His name is Jason Wright.

On the backwards tail, his answer was short. We solved this in 1974.
A comet called Kohoutek did exactly the same thing. Heavy grains of dust roll off the sunlit side and fall behind. From our angle, that trailing dust lines up — and looks like a spike aimed at the Sun.
It's not a tail. It's a trick of perspective. And we watched another comet do it just a few years ago.
Spooky to look at. Fifty years old to explain.
